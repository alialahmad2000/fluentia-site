# Why the live homepage feels weak (audit, 2026-11-24)

Measured on fluentia.academy `/` («الفجر», NextLanding) at 390×844 and 1440×900.
Shots: `before/` (home-m-sheet.jpg / home-d-sheet.jpg = the whole page at a glance).

## 1. There are no people
- 24 phone screens of page, **zero humans**. Every visual is a UI screenshot inside a phone frame on navy.
- The page shows the *tool* and never the *result*: nobody sees a Saudi employee speaking calmly in a meeting.
  The visitor has to imagine the payoff themselves. A premium brand shows it.
- The same phone mockup appears ~8 times (hero, platform, 4-step carousel, «وأكثر», plan). By the third it is wallpaper.

## 2. Hierarchy and pacing
- Mobile page = **20,269 px** (24 screens), desktop 18,307 px. Long, and every section has the same weight:
  giant headline → small grey paragraph → phone. Nothing tells the eye "this is the important part".
- Headlines are huge, body copy is tiny and grey-on-navy (low contrast, hard to read on a phone in daylight).
- Five packages with dense feature lists arrive before any proof that this works. Decision fatigue before trust.
- The hero has three competing actions (book, tour, free level test) plus a logo strip, a phone and a sun.

## 3. Copy
- «نزّل ما يمنعك من امتلاك الإنجليزية» is abstract and reads like a translation; a Saudi wouldn't say it.
- «بين الحصص», «العائق ليس أنت» and «الأرقام كما هي» are clever titles that need their paragraph to make sense.
- The numbers section shows counters (+8,686 answers, +11,980 minutes) without a frame a visitor can feel.

## 4. Visual language
- Glows, a gradient sun, a moon, soft orbs and blurred light behind the phones: all against the flat-colour rule.
- One navy for the whole page, so sections blur into each other. No rhythm of dark/light, wide/tight, photo/type.
- The `/journal` «معرض» direction (flat ink/paper + one loud accent, giant Alexandria type, numbered plates,
  real photographs) is far stronger and is the taste signal to follow.

## 5. Trust
- One student story (Malak). Gov logos are tiny and low-contrast in the hero.
- Real trainer, academic coach, live classes: the strongest differentiators are text bullets, never *seen*.

## 6. Mobile / performance
- Lab LCP ~2.9 s (the hero phone image). CLS 0.007 mobile / 0.048 desktop (desktop close to the 0.05 line).
- The header CTA pill plus «القائمة» is good and stays.

## What the rebuild does about it
1. **People first**: every section is carried by real-looking Saudi footage (Higgsfield), with one recurring cast.
2. **One promise per screen**, short Saudi lines, big readable body (≥17 px on phones), high contrast.
3. **Flat ink / paper / one accent** (brand cyan #00F9FE), giant type, numbered chapters, after the `/journal` taste.
4. **Shorter**: 8 chapters, the platform shown once (one interactive showcase), no pricing tables on this page.
5. **One action**: «احجز لقاءك المبدئي المجاني» everywhere, plus WhatsApp at the end.
6. **Proof slots** clearly marked for real stories; nothing invented.
