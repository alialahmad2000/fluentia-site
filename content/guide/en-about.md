Fluentia Academy (أكاديمية طلاقة) is a Saudi online English academy for adults, with one-to-one programmes for employees and professionals. Classes are live on Google Meet, in a small group of up to 7 students at the same level or one-to-one. Five monthly packages start at SAR 500, and everything starts with a free initial meeting before payment.

## Who is Fluentia Academy for?

Fluentia Academy teaches adults. The one-to-one programmes are aimed at professionals and anyone who wants a curriculum built around them. The goals offered at sign-up are:

- foundation from zero,
- improving conversation,
- IELTS preparation,
- English for work,
- study or a scholarship abroad,
- travel and daily life.

In the one-to-one programmes the curriculum is built around the learner's field; examples shown on the site include nursing, accounting, engineering, IT, marketing and teaching.

The academy is also explicit about who it does not suit: people looking for a shortcut without daily commitment, people on a very tight budget (free options such as Duolingo, YouTube and ChatGPT are good), people who prefer large classes (20+), people who expect to learn without regular practice, and people who want a quick certificate rather than real fluency.

| Intensive private training (التدريب الفردي المكثّف) | SAR 3,000 | 12 private classes a month (3 a week) with a dedicated teacher, plus 8 practice sessions a month (2 a week) with an academic mentor, i.e. 5 meetings a week; extended diagnostic session; curriculum designed for the learner's field; full platform; everything in Private training |

The academic mentor in the intensive programme is usually a native English speaker and is a different person from the teacher. The SAR 2,000 programme includes no mentor and no practice sessions. ## What are the payment, cancellation and refund rules?

- Payment is monthly, in advance, at the start of each Gregorian month.
- Prices are in SAR and include everything in the chosen package.
- No long-term commitment: a subscription can stop at the end of any month.
- Cancel any time: the next month is not charged; the current month counts.
- Full refund within 7 days of the start of the month, only if no class has been attended. After any class, no refund.
- Package changes (up or down) take effect at the start of the next month; no mid-month price change.
- The academy does not store card data; payments are processed by payment providers.
- The apps contain no payments; subscriptions are handled with the academy team.
- No guarantee of a specific level or IELTS band: results depend on the learner's commitment and practice.
- The terms are governed by Saudi law, with the Riyadh courts having jurisdiction.

## How do you start at Fluentia Academy?

1. **Register a name and phone number** on the website (about 30 seconds). The academy replies on WhatsApp within hours. For the one-to-one programmes, a consultation is booked on [the private training page](/private).
2. **Free initial meeting** with the trainer, before any payment and with no commitment.
3. **Level, goal and schedule** are identified in that meeting. The free [placement test](/level-test) is optional and its result can be sent on WhatsApp to get a plan.
4. **Track and package** are recommended. For Private training, the number of weekly classes and therefore the price is agreed here.
5. **Monthly payment, first classes and platform access.** Classes start at the beginning of each Gregorian month; learners can join any time and the trainer makes up what was missed. The platform is open from day one.
6. **Follow-up and progress.** Daily follow-up outside class happens on Telegram; assessments are monthly in Asas and weekly in Tamayuz.

The academy expects 3 to 5 hours a week of learner time (in and outside class), and at least 3 months to see a real difference.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Fluentia Academy online or in person?
Fully online. Live classes run on Google Meet, and the learning platform is at app.fluentia.academy.

### How much does Fluentia Academy cost per month?
Self-study SAR 500, Asas SAR 750, Tamayuz SAR 1,500, Private training from SAR 2,000, and Intensive private training SAR 3,000 per month.

### How many students are in a group class?
A maximum of 7, all at the same level.

### Do I have to pay before the first meeting?
No. The initial meeting is free, comes before any payment, and carries no commitment.

### Can I get a refund?
Yes: a full refund within 7 days of the start of the month, provided no class has been attended. After any class there is no refund, but cancellation is possible at any time and the next month is not charged.

### Is Fluentia Academy the same as fluentia.us?
No. Fluentia Academy (fluentia.academy) is an independent Saudi academy with no connection to any other organisation named "Fluentia".

### Does Fluentia guarantee a level or an IELTS band?
No. Results depend on the learner's commitment and practice, and the terms state that no specific level or band is guaranteed.
